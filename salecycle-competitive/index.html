Executive summary

The personalisation engine market has moved from a tactical A/B-testing add-on to mission-critical infrastructure: a $1.2B market in 2024 growing ~21% a year toward a projected $31.6B by 2030. The top of the field is crowded with three Gartner Leaders (Insider One, Bloomreach, Dynamic Yield) that win on either channel breadth or commerce-catalogue depth. None of them lead with identity. That is the opening. SaleCycle, with Beyable, owns a different sentence: identity resolution plus real-time personalisation, GDPR-compliant without third-party cookies, in a market where the cookie is dying. The proof exists (a reported 70% identification of anonymous traffic, Breitling at 45%, a 30% conversion uplift). The gap is not the product. It is that the merger is real on paper but not yet in one brand, one story, one platform that a buyer can see, and the AI visibility that is starting to build is converting nothing yet.

6. The wedge: one technology, three modules

The SaleCycle + Beyable thesis in one line: identity without personalisation is wasted, personalisation without identity is guesswork. The merged platform closes that loop. This is the story the competitors cannot easily copy, because the identity layer is the hard part.

REACH

Identify

Gather audience data in full GDPR compliance and identify visitors without third-party cookies. The entry point, and the technical moat in a cookieless era.

RETAIN

Personalise on-site

Real-time behavioural scoring while the visitor is still on the page, adapting the experience to the individual the moment it matters (the Beyable layer).

RE-ENGAGE

Recover off-site

Win back lost sales and build loyalty through Email, SMS, RCS and WhatsApp after the visitor leaves (the original SaleCycle strength).

What the data says

Six reads that set where SaleCycle's marketing energy should go.

01

The top is crowded, but on the wrong axis

Three Gartner Leaders compete on channel breadth or catalogue depth. None of them lead with identity resolution. SaleCycle's category is adjacent, not inside theirs, which means it is a positioning fight, not a feature arms race.

02

Identity + cookieless is the real moat

Reach identifies a reported ~70% of anonymous traffic, GDPR-native, no third-party cookies. As the cookie dies, that is a durable technical advantage the Leaders don't put first. It should be the headline, not a feature buried in module three.

03

The proof exists but isn't packaged

Breitling at 45% identity resolution, a 30% conversion uplift, 8% higher basket value. Strong, board-ready numbers, currently scattered across decks rather than front-and-centre as the reason to believe.

04

AI visibility is trending up, converting zero

From January to May, AI-surfaced visits rose on a positive trend but none converted to a lead of any kind. The presence is forming; the capture mechanism behind it isn't built yet. That's a system gap, not a content gap.

05

The merger is real on paper, not in the market

Competitors present one coherent platform. SaleCycle + Beyable still reads as two: one brand, one story, one platform that a buyer can see don't fully exist yet. Closing that is the highest-leverage marketing move before any channel spend.

06

Don't fight breadth or spend, fight on the wedge

Insider's 12+ channels and Bloomreach's catalogue depth are won battles. The win is the identity wedge plus the service-inclusive TCO story, packaged sharply and run by a lean team with AI doing the heavy lifting.
