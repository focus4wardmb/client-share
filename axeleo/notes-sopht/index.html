Sopht: a first read.

Public sources only · 29 May 2026 · 2 min read

01 · Observations

What's visible right now

Three things noticed from 20 minutes of public reading.

Three category words across three surfaces. The homepage now reads "ITOps through sustainability." Google still shows the older title "Plateforme Green IT." Partner and analyst posts call it "GreenOps." Three frames, three buyer types.

The customer list is the strongest asset. BNP Paribas, VINCI, VEOLIA, ADECCO, EDENRED. Logo concentration like that is rare in B2B SaaS. Current messaging leads with product features rather than customer proof.

The regulatory tailwind isn't being used. CSRD scope-3 reporting (rolling out 2025-2028) and France's REEN law (annual measurement and reduction of digital footprint for large companies) both push enterprises to do what Sopht does. Neither appears in current messaging.

02 · Questions

Three questions

Three questions worth asking first.

Category. ITOps, GreenOps, or Green IT? Three frames are in play. They reach different buyers. Picking one in 2026 changes who you sell to.

Buyer. CIO, CFO, or Sustainability Officer? Those logos suggest several people sign off. The one who actually controls the budget is rarely the one being spoken to in current messaging.

Regulation. CSRD and REEN are both running tailwinds 2024-2028. Either a deliberate choice not to lead with them, or a missed opportunity.
