01 · What I heard

Positive, in your words

A suite, not a collection of tools. Around ten acquisitions since 2023, one platform, the ambition of a European alternative to HubSpot, Salesforce or Klaviyo. Positive User since May: User.com, Sarbacane, Mailingwork and 4Dem on one foundation. €70M ARR, 45,000 customers.

The brand is not settled. Merge under Positive User while keeping the high-value local domains, and the fear of redirects. positivegroup.com points to User, which points to user.com. It needs a real brand strategy and a rollout plan. Hard, but worth it.

The 40,000 Sarbacane customers stay on account.sarbacane.com, no forced migration. They are the ones living the name change first.

The marketing organisation. A team spread across several European cities, a CMO leaving on 28 September, a merger reorganisation carried out with determination, whose second half, bringing the teams along, is still to do.

What you expect. Leadership, structure, a complete reorganisation. A first phase as a fractional CMO, and we talk again.

02 · What I see

What the site already says

Three brands, three states. sarbacane.com redirects to user.com/fr, with a "Sarbacane becomes Positive User" page. 4dem.it redirects to user.com/it/4dem. mailingwork.de lives its own life: brand untouched, not one mention of Positive beyond the careers link. Three different decisions, taken at three different moments. A German customer and an Italian customer don't see the same company.

The architecture is already chosen, without having been decided. Positive User, Positive Surfer, Positive Signitic, Positive Iconosquare, Positive rapidmail, Positive noCRM: six products, one prefix. That is an umbrella brand endorsing product brands. The choice has consequences for the domains (the prefix lives on positivegroup.com, the application on user.com), for SEO, and for the promise. It deserves to be written down, not only applied.

Two promises. positivegroup.com: "Turning reach into relationship". user.com/fr: "Faites de chaque contact le début d'une histoire". Close, not identical. And neither carries the "AI built in, no prompts to master" you described to me. The most differentiating message is on no homepage.

The question that settles half of it: is Positive a group brand that endorses products, or a product brand that absorbs the others? Today, the site does both.

The useful precedent. Sendinblue became Brevo in 2023, with the same questions: the domains, the legacy customers, a multi-country team. I ran that change from A to Z. I know where it breaks, and in what order you keep it from breaking.

03 · The audit

Two subjects, one decision

01
Brand architecture

Scope: the six brands, the domains, the 40,000 Sarbacane accounts, the five countries. Deliverable: an architecture recommendation (umbrella, endorsed or single brand), a rollout plan per brand and per domain (what gets redirected, what stays, in which order), and the line each homepage will carry.

02
Marketing organisation

Scope: the current team, its cities, its skills, what each country expects from the centre. Deliverable: the target model (a central function with local relays, the shape I worked in at Criteo, then Brevo), the roles and their remit, the sequence to get there, and the change management handled at the same time as the org chart.

The deliverable is a decision document, not a report. You come out with clear choices made, and the plan to hold them.
