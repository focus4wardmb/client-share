01 · Executive summary to finalise

World-class technology.
The engine to carry it doesn't exist yet.

€1M revenue target 6-12 mo · ~30% there 0 deals lost to a competitor since founding 6-12 mo cold sales cycle 2 paying clients: Safran · EDF English-first · US-first, locked

The story is told at half size

Both founders already tell the engineer-centred story in the room: empowerment, out of the parametric prison. The public surface says "1000x faster" like everyone else. The positioning can be sharper, and pushed much further.

Measurement is dark

A GA4 tag fires on the site, but nobody can act on it: no access shared, both Search Console properties unvalidated, no tag manager, no conversion defined. Data collects; nothing steers. Finding and fix in one.

The proof is trapped

Safran benchmark, 98% accuracy vs Google/DeepMind/Caltech reference models, NeurIPS submission, Boeing/Airbus pilots: all locked inside a fundraising PDF no buyer will ever see.

Zero lost deals is not safety. It's a category nobody has claimed yet. The definition is being written right now by players with $170M+ war chests, and this is an equipment market: seats filled today stay filled. When European buyers come in-market, they'll ask for whoever educated them. NPco can't outspend that cohort. It needs the right story, and the engine that tells it at scale, before the window closes.
02 · In your words

Your framing, in your own words.
If this reads right, the audit read you right.

"Les clients font de la conception paramétrique... c'est une prison pour eux. Ils ont besoin de pouvoir en sortir."Emmanuel · Aug 6 · the product philosophy in one line

Empowerment, not sovereignty

Eric's doc says "Physics Sovereignty"; Emmanuel chose empowerment: master the physics of your product without paying to test it. The customer word wins.

400 → 1,200 → 400,000

The buyer's stated need: clear the simulation backlog (1,200 vs 400). The real capacity: 400,000. From "catch up" to "explore what you'd never have tried".

Model-first, on-premise

Clients build and own their models; deployment is on-prem, not cloud. In an industry paranoid about design data, this is a trust asset the story underuses.

The strategy is already locked where it matters: public positioning stays global, GTM execution targets matrix cells (vertical × physics), English-first, US-first. "Tu peux enlever French et Inria."

03 · The buyer & the motion

Experts first. Then the top.
Never the middle.

The motion that wins: technical validation with the pure expert, then straight to the CTO with "your own expert validated this". At Air Liquide the engineer next to Emmanuel called the executive committee of a €20B group when something was wrong. In French industrial groups, the expert IS the influencer.

The champion

The simulation engineer / technical expert. Falls in love on the benchmark, validates, escalates.

The signature

Program director → BU director (Safran pattern). Ambition: comex-level strategic buys.

The cycle

6-12 months cold; much faster when already known in the group. Almost no RFPs: this is a need-creation market, which is precisely what marketing is for.

Matthieu, Aug 13, confirms the motion independently, and sharpens it: the champion must be influent, not just convinced. The known failure mode is winning the engineer while the information never reaches the budget holder. His value angle for the champion: he shines inside his organisation, designing products he could never have imagined. And the interface is about to change: application engineers now being recruited will become the main client-facing layer, and the champion playbook needs to survive that handoff.
04 · Positioning & category

Everyone says "1000x faster".
Nobody puts the engineer in the sentence.

PhysicsX: "Accelerating Industrial Innovation with AI". Emmi: "Large Engineering Models". Luminary: "Physics. Encoded in AI." The convergence is total: technology as hero, speed as proof. "The optimal design already exists" has the same flaw: no one in the sentence designs anything.

The open seat

The engineer-centred story: escape the parametric prison, explore what you could never test, change what engineering dares to try. Empowerment (Emmanuel's word) against everyone else's automation story. Matthieu lands on the same word unprompted: "empowering the engineers", not replacing them; both founders already tell this story; only the public surface doesn't.

The trust pillar

Every buyer will ask "when do I trust the surrogate?" Matthieu's answer is already built into the product: a confidence index on every prediction, and a clean line: NPco doesn't replace certified simulation, it explores the design space upstream so you validate one final design instead of 50–100. Say it publicly, first, and own the conversation. NPco's own NAFEMS/Teratec survey proves the stakes: trust & validation is the #3 adoption barrier (47%), and only 26.2% of teams exploring AI are satisfied. Market maturity helps: buyers no longer ask whether AI is useful, only whether NPco solves their problem.

"Candidate altitude: faster and safer products, not faster engineers. More of the design space explored means better, cleaner, safer machines, sooner. Two rooms: the engineer hears freedom, the comex hears competitive advantage."Working hypothesis · validate with both founders before it goes public
05 · Brand architecture

Augur, NPco, augursim.ai.
Three names for one company.

The legal entity is AUGUR SAS. Email lives at npco.ai. The Value Messaging doc's footer says augursim.ai and titles the baseline "NPco". The fundraising deck is fully Augur-branded. Enterprise trust is built on coherence; today the first Google check breaks it, and the first AI check is worse: Perplexity resolves "NPCO" to a Saudi aviation-services company and "Augur" to a robotics digital twin. Whichever name wins needs an entity footprint that owns it.

Decide the name

Complete the Augur→NPco transition or reverse it, but on purpose, before the US push. Renames only get more expensive.

One domain

Consolidate email, site and documents on the chosen domain; redirect the rest.

One boilerplate

One company description, everywhere: site, deck, LinkedIn, PR, papers. V1 to react to: "NPco builds AI physics models that let engineers explore the full design space at the speed of their ideas. Founded by Inria researchers, its models run on-premise, learn from your simulations, and let you validate one final design instead of fifty. Safran and EDF already engineer with NPco." V1 · validate at restitution

One decision hiding inside this one: the flag. Emmanuel's instruction was "tu peux enlever French et Inria" (US-first, global framing). Matthieu, independently, names European and French sovereignty as a differentiator to integrate. Both can be true (sovereignty as a sales argument in EU accounts, invisible in the US surface), but that split has to be chosen, not left to drift. To resolve with both founders at restitution.
