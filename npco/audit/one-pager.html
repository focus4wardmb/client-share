What the audit found

Empowerment, trust, ownership: the four pillars are true and provable. Everything public still leads with speed, like every competitor.

The proof is hidden

Safran benchmark, NeurIPS submission, Boeing/Airbus pilots: locked in a fundraising PDF. Where buyers and AI engines look, the established players are the answer.

The four brand pillars
1 · Empowerment. The engineer at the centre, out of the parametric prison.
2 · Dare to go beyond. The design you never had time or budget to simulate.
3 · Trust in AI. A confidence index on every prediction; AI replaces iterations, never final verification.
4 · Yours, not rented. Trained on your simulations, run on your machines, the model is yours, IP included.
