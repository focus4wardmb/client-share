05 · The diagnosis

Trust. Process. Data.

This is the headline finding. Fix this layer and the rest compounds. Skip it, and the tactical fixes don't stick because the function underneath them keeps breaking the work.

Trust

Sales doesn't believe marketing produces qualified leads. Product doesn't believe marketing reads the roadmap. The CEO sees the gap and the marketing team sees the gap. Nobody has named it out loud yet.

Process

No shared handoff between Sales, Marketing, and Ops. Lead routing is manual. Content validation is ad-hoc. Events get planned without before/during/after structure. Every successful play requires a hero.

Data

HubSpot is a CMS, not a marketing automation tool. Salesforce sync is one-way. No lead scoring, no enrichment, no behavioural data. Attribution to source is impossible. Decisions get made on hunches.

Every other pillar in this audit, brand, lead gen, product marketing, search, has tactical recommendations that work only when this trust-process-data layer is moving. Build the foundation first.

07 · Priority timeline

Here is what I recommend focusing on. This list is by order of priority.

Three horizons. Each block has 2 to 3 named actions. The first 30 days fix the foundation. The next 90 build the brand and product marketing layer. The 12-month horizon is the compounding engine.

Immediate

First 30 daysFoundation. Restore trust.

  • Lead Gen process redesign. Lead enrichment tool live. Lead scoring with two flows. SF integration cleaned.
  • Sales ⇔ MKT alignment kickoff. Lead definition agreed. Handoff process documented. Weekly sync established.
  • Event-process redesign. Apply on next event: before / during / after structure with KPIs.
Mid-term

Next 90 daysBrand and product marketing.

  • Brand platform rebuild. Messaging framework aligned across CEO / MKT / Sales / Product. TOV, design refresh, brand book.
  • Product marketing foundation. Buyer persona research. Pricing & packaging revisited. Solutions / features matrix recreated.
  • Lead nurture programs. Non-MQLs into structured nurture sequences. Win-loss analysis loop.
Long-term

12 monthsCompounding engine.

  • SEO non-brand engine. Content production aligned to SEO from day one. Paid complementing organic, not substituting.
  • Team scale. Hire specialists. External partners for PR and content distribution. Function reaches 6%+ of headcount.
  • Sales-MKT-Product as operating rhythm. Roadmap aligned. Trust restored by repeated delivery.

Each horizon depends on the one before it. Skipping foundation work and starting with brand or SEO produces a deck that looks better and a pipeline that still doesn't move.

C1 · Pillar

Website

Clean design, but text-heavy and not optimised for the buyer journey. Mobile and US localisation are the two biggest gaps.

AObservations & recommendations

UX, UI, content

  • Clean and modern design, yet generic and unmemorable.
  • The website is text-heavy, making it difficult to focus on the key message.
  • The client carousel is very good, but other proof points should be more prominent.
  • No pricing page (potential conversion lift, ref: Mews).
  • The website has a chatbot, but it is offline.
  • URLs still need improvement for SEO.
  • Success stories should be in the main navigation; solutions don't include case studies.
  • The website is not optimised for mobile (41.8% of sessions are mobile).
  • English only, while 67% of revenue comes from Europe and the US is not optimised.

Web performance + lead generation

  • About 5K users per month, 95% new users. Main geos: US, FR, DE, UK, India.
  • Traffic: Direct 57%, organic search 20%, referral 6%, organic social (LinkedIn) 8%, paid social 6%, other 3%.
  • Organic search drives more engaged sessions than direct (41% vs 34%) and higher avg time (1m02), 7.01 events/session.
  • January: 24 leads + 13 prospects (most from direct traffic). Booking calendar inconsistent, no confirmation email.
  • Form not visible above the fold. No scoring, no enrichment.
Continue
  • Generate customer testimonials and feature them on the site.
Stop / Start
  • Reduce copy and polish, focus on key messages.
  • Improve design and platform shots; add UI moments and videos.
  • Improve mobile experience.
  • Test CTAs, copy, and layout.
  • Move key items to main navigation.
  • Fix SEO technical issues, fix URLs.
  • Consider adding a product tour or a demo.
  • Create a multi-region / multi-language site.
  • Allow non-work emails on contact form.

C2 · Pillar

Brand

A "next-generation" positioning that the brand identity doesn't yet live up to. Three missing elements: reassurance, momentum, movement.

BObservations & recommendations

Positioning + messaging

  • "Next generation" is very good. Thynk is identified as a disruptor and fosters innovation.
  • Three key elements missing: reassurance, momentum, movement.
  • Reassurance: needs to be perceived as a trusted advisor. Proof points (results, quotes) need strategic placement.
  • Movement: Thynk is uniquely positioned to drive customer-centric digitalisation in hospitality. The "bigger than us" opportunity isn't fully reflected.
  • Momentum: hoteliers need to feel the urgency: if they don't jump on the spacecraft, they'll be left behind.
  • For advanced users: everyone talks AI. Without data, AI is impossible.

Look & feel + tone of voice

  • Recently rebranded, more "put together," yet not polished.
  • Doesn't convey high-end, market-leading, world-class.
  • Discrepancy between "next-gen" and brand elements (icons) that are archaic.
  • Slides cluttered, suggesting a complex solution.
  • TOV is corporate and professional, but not memorable or unique.
  • Word choice: "reshaped" or "transformed" beats "converted"; "enable" or "empower" beats "help."
Continue
  • Keep "next generation" as the core positioning angle.
  • Keep using action verbs that create dynamic rhythm.
Stop / Start
  • Restructure and refocus the brand narrative.
  • Recreate essential brand elements: mission, vision, USP, tagline.
  • Polish copy using a professional copywriter.
  • Revise design to be modern, crisp, ENT-worthy.
  • Introduce captivating content: UI moments and product videos.
  • Create a brand book, TOV and style guide.

C3 · Pillar

Lead generation

Branded keywords work; non-branded ranks have been flat for 16 months. Events absorb 74% of budget without process or ROI tracking.

CObservations & recommendations

General + SEO + paid search

  • 23 leads in Jan (40% from search), 18 leads in Feb (60% from search).
  • Affiliate content like HotelTechReport not present.
  • Content often produced without a clear utilisation plan; often without SEO in mind.
  • SEO is not a priority (SEMrush or SpyFu not used).
  • Non-brand keywords flat for 16 months. Branded keywords improving on impressions, clicks, CTRs.
  • Test campaign generated learning but lacked impact (3 leads): inadequate budget, not geo-optimised, no conversion tracking, one-keyword board match, ill-defined objectives.

Paid LinkedIn (ABM) + events

  • ABM running on targeted accounts in the US, but not including all relevant titles.
  • ~40 events globally per year, spend over €200K (74% of total budget).
  • Event process not designed to maximise outcome; ROI not calculated or tracked.
  • One e-book campaign per quarter; campaigns not always linked to sales priorities or ABM.
Continue
  • Run relevant events in key sales areas.
  • Maintain SEO positions on branded keywords.
Stop / Start
  • Double down on SEO non-brand.
  • Invest wisely in SEA.
  • Treat events as campaigns, not isolated activities.
  • Improve and enhance ABM programs.
  • Add lead scoring with two flows: low-qualified (watch a demo) and high-qualified (book a meeting).
  • Enrich Salesforce data with web data via third-party tools.

C6 · Pillar

Team, process & data

The headline finding. There is lack of trust, process, and data, and this gap holds growth back more than any tactical fix.

FObservations & recommendations

Marketing team & budget

  • Severely understaffed: 3% of workforce (6%+ at competitors). Smallest growth projection to 2025.
  • Budget limited, includes full-time staff. Limited third-party support.
  • Marketing strategy is "light," not data-driven. Unclear how success or ROI are measured.
  • Lack of trust between marketing team and its stakeholders.
  • Team lacks some areas of expertise; mentoring and training will help.

Processes + data

  • Need clear processes between Sales ⇔ MKT ⇔ Product: LG, content validation, events, product roadmap.
  • US form sends an email (performance unguaranteed); EU form books a meeting directly (scalability unguaranteed).
  • Inbound leads not scored or segmented. Event leads followed up manually.
  • Missing documentation that becomes critical as team scales.
  • Tech stack siloed: tools not integrated. HubSpot mostly a CMS, not leveraged by LG.
  • Salesforce → HubSpot one-way only. Contacts not enriched with behavioural data. Campaign field unused.
Continue
  • The pieces that work (LinkedIn cadence, HubSpot baseline): fix the integration, don't rip the tool.
Stop / Start
  • Establish clear, effective processes for event, LG, validation, sales requests.
  • Solve the data problem by utilising HubSpot, or replace it as a MAP (not as a CMS).
  • Add data enrichment and lead scoring.
  • Organise the nomenclature used by teams.
  • Enhance collaboration between teams to restore trust.
