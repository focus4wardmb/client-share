The corridor holds on three pre-launch decisions: a sales-side owner activates engagement signals within 48h (without one it falls to 1–2 /qtr, and no media budget compensates), the ICP-meeting definition is written and validated before launch, and the ramp is respected: month 1 setup, first honestly attributable meetings in months 3–4. With your 5-6 month cycle, the first ABM-attributable signature lands around Q2 2027. Sept–Dec produces pipeline, not revenue. Worth agreeing on now, not in December.

The spend → output answer. 5–9 meetings a quarter at your 10–25% conversion is 0.5–2 signatures a quarter, at ~€265K each. The media behind it: €25–35K total Sept–Dec, inside the €5–10K/month line already in the budget fourchettes. One signature funds roughly three years of the program; break-even is one deal every 18 months. Steer on that: a meetings count gets contested every month, the unit economics don't.
